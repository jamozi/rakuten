## Objective and boundary

Implement only ST-1907 as a reversible, local, provider-neutral,
recorded/synthetic, default-disabled portfolio-optimization seam. The slice may
evaluate already-classified evidence and emit deterministic strengthen,
consolidate, or withdraw metadata for human review. It may not activate,
approve, apply, rank recommendations, mutate content, publish, call a provider,
use credentials, persist, stage, release, or act in Production.

5. Run pinned owner generation/check, tests, formatting/linting, strict type

## Hard stops and completion

Secrets, credentials, personal/Production data, provider or user-facing
writes, publication, staging, release, Production, irreversible operations,
safety-boundary weakening, invented Open Decisions, and unresolvable ownership
collisions are hard stops. Local completion requires deterministic owner
generation/check, green owned tests/static checks, no introduced debt, one
clean commit, and an unchanged Canonical/status registry. Formal TST-032, real
observations, the Product Owner decision, separate release decision, staging,
release, and Production remain outside this plan.
