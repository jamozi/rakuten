# ST-0306 execution plan — Database roles and grants

## Authority and scope

Implement the approved ST-0306 Story from the canonical backlog, RAOS-SEC-001,
the hash-pinned upstream role SQL, and the finalized ST-0004 RLS policy source.
The Story has no open decision. ST-0303 and ST-0305 are its installed
predecessors.

## Plan

1. Pin and validate the exact eight-role upstream source and the 22 finalized
   policies already assigned to ST-0306.
2. Generate one transactional Alembic revision, a machine catalog, validation
   SQL, and a hash-closed manifest.
3. Advance the checksum catalog/runner while retaining predecessor structural
   validation and adding exact role, grant, default-ACL, ownership, and policy
   validation.
4. Prove TST-011 locally on exact PostgreSQL 18.4, including public isolation,
   non-owner application roles, immutable-data guards, downgrade, and forward
   recovery.

## Boundaries

No login roles, credentials, external memberships, missing downstream tables,
retention rules, provider adapters, publication, or production operation.
Cluster-global group roles and the monotonic default function-`EXECUTE` denial
for `PUBLIC` survive downgrade; workload authority does not.

## Outcome

- Advanced the cumulative graph to `202608030006` with exactly eight
  `NOLOGIN` workload roles and the reviewed schema/table/function ACL matrix.
- Installed the exact 22 ST-0306 policies with RLS enabled and forced on all
  11 predecessor tables, while deferring five grants whose `ops` relations do
  not yet exist.
- Revoked PostgreSQL's owner-global default `PUBLIC EXECUTE` for functions.
  The global deny protects all current managed schemas and future schemas
  owned by the migration principal, and deliberately survives downgrade.
- Added exact in-transaction and standalone catalog postconditions plus local
  PostgreSQL 18.4 negative/runtime coverage for role drift, public isolation,
  immutable facts, downgrade, and recovery.

Formal TST-011, hosted CI, migration-owner review, human review, release, and
production execution remain separate and `NOT_EXECUTED`.
