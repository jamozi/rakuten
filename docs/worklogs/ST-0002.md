### Objective and boundaries

The story converts the accepted Job-state design delta into a formal,
hash-pinned revision bundle without overwriting the v0.1 packages or applying
the proposal SQL directly.

The final monorepo/contracts tree, reusable migration runner, canonical
IAM/OPS install, queue fake, dispatcher/worker runtime, CI, staging, production
database, and release action belong to later stories and are not hidden inside
this change.

### Ambiguities and safe decisions

- The v0.1 package is a non-deployed baseline candidate. The narrowed Job
  status enum is classified as a pre-release canonical defect correction, not
  a claim about compatibility with already published consumers.
- `job_version` is the Job message/schema version and remains distinct from
  optimistic concurrency `lock_version`.
- `cancel_requested_at` stays in persistence/Admin representations and is not
  added to the worker Job Message.
- The approved 16 transitions are preserved exactly. The missing expiry edges
  from retry states and Quarantine-release timestamp behavior are documented
  for `ST-1404`; no new transition was invented.
- Full TST-013 queue behavior cannot exist before its queue/runtime-owning
  stories. This story validates the exact state graph and guard contract only.

## Proposal defects corrected

The proposal is retained as provenance and never executed as the migration.
The formal revision corrects:

1. final `status` default `PENDING` versus a canonical-only constraint, by
   setting the Contract default to `REQUESTED`;
2. incorrect constraint name `ck_ops_job_terminal_time`, by replacing the real
   baseline `ck_ops_job_completion`;
3. a single destructive update, by using explicit Expand, Migrate, and Contract
   phases;
4. incorrect MIG-002 lineage, by targeting the MIG-001 `ops.job` foundation;
5. potential collision with baseline `ck_ops_job_version`, by preserving its
   `lock_version >= 0` check and adding
   `ck_ops_job_version_positive` separately;
6. unsafe lossy downgrade, by refusing canonical-only states or meaningful new
   field values before mutation and freezing writers with an
   `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` lock;
7. three inherited resource-catalog pointers to a nonexistent
   `#/components/schemas` tree, by deterministically normalizing them to their
   actual `#/public_resources` definitions.
8. long `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` locks across full-table constraint validation, by
   separating metadata DDL, validation, and finalization checkpoints;
9. a nominal 1,000-row loop whose locks/WAL accumulated in one transaction, by
   making one invocation one committed at-most-1,000-row batch with
   `remaining_rows` and per-state checkpoint output;
10. same-name but wrong-definition revision indexes, by checking validity,
    readiness, table, access method, key order, and predicate through exact
    PostgreSQL index definitions before Contract;
11. Job retry/cancel metadata that required a success ETag without declaring
    the response header, by adding the ETag `$ref` to OPS-003/OPS-004 success
    responses and testing the whole concurrency contract;
12. a one-level-short nested Job-state contract link, by resolving custom
    contract links from each generated artifact;
13. generator deletion/custom-output hazards, by restricting CLI output to the
    owned bundle, staging the full result before replacement, verifying the
    ownership marker, and restoring the prior tree on generation/install
    failure.
