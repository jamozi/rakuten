# ST-0605 implementation worklog

## 2026-08-24 — recorded local Claim/evidence runtime V1

- Preserved the historical non-executable reference plan and added an
  independent executable recorded-synthetic runtime.
- Kept policy, persistence, and AI Claim vocabularies distinct.  No inferred
  cross-namespace conversion was introduced and ST-0308 repositories were not
  widened.
- Added Article/body to approved Packet Version/content binding plus independent
  Claim-inventory, Packet membership, Fact-validation, conflict-closure, and
  Product/Variant identity receipts. Each receipt pins owner Story, contract
  version/hash, subject/input/decision hash, and time; V1 accepts recorded
  synthetic receipts only.
- Kept ST-0602/ST-0603/ST-0504/ST-0803/ST-0804/ST-1401/ST-0805 owner semantics
  outside ST-0605. Missing or mismatched owner receipts are `UNEVALUABLE`, not
  guessed validation.
- The evaluator closes the Claim/Facts/links/Sources/snapshots/identity/
  conflicts/citations/attestations sets before counting. Its result remains a
  non-authoritative classification of the supplied DTO. The recording boundary
  independently resolves and hash-checks its preloaded complete input, so a
  self-declared Claim subset cannot become a trusted receipt. A different
  Packet, wrong Product/Variant, future source or Packet-approval evidence, and
  resolution without immutable review evidence fail closed.
- Implemented valid-`SUPPORTS`-only numerators, exact major equality, integer
  95% cross multiplication (including a 19/20 passing boundary), and
  zero-denominator `UNEVALUABLE`.
- Implemented explicit negative paths for qualifier-only, contradiction,
  packet escape, identity mismatch, staleness, open conflict, prohibited
  origin, tier mismatch, citation failure, missing type proof, unknown-value
  imputation, and predictive default blocking.
- Added evaluator-version/time/full-input/report hashes and semantic report
  coherence validation. The recording service resolves the Article Version
  through its trusted preloaded reader and the adapter checks and re-evaluates
  the constructor-time full-input digest before append, so a substituted
  `PASS` or post-construction object-alias mutation cannot enter the recorder.
  Append history retains immutable digest/canonical-byte values rather than
  caller report objects. The pure evaluator has no authority, and recorded
  attestation decision hashes are corruption checks rather than authentication.
- Added read-only evaluation and internal append-only recording ports/services,
  a bounded ENV-DEV/CI adapter, strict recorded-fixture decoder, deterministic
  owner generator with exact nested-contract validation and BaseException-safe
  transactional rollback plus retrying post-commit cleanup, and focused runtime
  tests. Persistent cleanup residue returns a closed diagnostic while retaining
  the already complete generated pair. Lazy package facades keep
  provider/generated siblings out of the fresh ST-0605 import closure; every
  loaded repository module plus `pyproject.toml`/`uv.lock` is hash-bound, and
  the generator enforces CPython 3.14.6, PyYAML 6.0.3, Pydantic 2.13.4,
  pydantic-core 2.46.4, and the locked pytest 9.1.1 verification tool.
- Regenerated the ST-0203, ST-0701, and ST-0801 owner manifests and propagated
  their exact predecessor hashes through the ST-0204, ST-0703, and ST-1204
  owner chains. These are mechanical provenance-only changes: executable
  semantics, fixture payloads, policy, authority, and status are unchanged.
- Verification results and exact source inventory are recorded in
  `changes/st-0605/LOCAL-IMPLEMENTATION-COMPLETION-20260824-v1.yaml` after the
