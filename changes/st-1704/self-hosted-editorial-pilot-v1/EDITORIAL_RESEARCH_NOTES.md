# Editorial and experience research record

Observed on 2026-08-23. These pages informed structure and interaction patterns only.
They are not product evidence, and no wording, score, ranking, review, or image is
reused in an article packet.

## Patterns adopted

- [ROOMIE](https://www.roomie.jp/) leads many stories with a recognisable daily
  friction and the change the reader wants. The pilot applies that pattern through a
  short `problem` block before specifications, while avoiding first-hand language.
- [Rentio PRESS](https://www.rentio.jp/matome/category/travel/suitcase/) groups travel
  choices by real constraints such as size, duration, storage, and opening style. The
  pilot applies the same decision principle through conditional conclusions and
  `condition` labels, without copying its claims or promoting rental inventory.
- [mybest](https://my-best.com/15) makes criteria and side-by-side differences
  scannable. The pilot applies that usability principle through a compact methodology,
  semantic comparison table, and consistent product cards. It does not import scores,
  rankings, reviews, or test results because this pilot performs no hands-on testing.
- [Wirecutter's recommendation policy](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/21870729656596-Wirecutter-Product-Recommendations)
  separates editorial decisions from affiliate relationships. The pilot makes that
  separation executable: recommendation order accepts specification and use-condition
  inputs only, while finance signals remain observation-only in the day-14 ledger.

## Resulting experience contract

Every article follows one reader journey: visible advertising disclosure, daily
problem, 30-second conditional conclusion, comparison criteria, comparison table,
photo product cards, unsuitable conditions, primary sources, and related articles.
Calls to action occur only in product cards and the closing conditional summary.

The first-view disclosure states that the site may receive affiliate compensation,
that compensation does not affect selection or order, and which editorial tasks used
AI assistance. Product claims remain bound only to official primary sources.

## Deliberate exclusions

- no universal winner, numerical score, or affiliate-economics ranking;
- no competitor wording, test result, review text, product image, or evaluation;
- no fixed price, inventory, points, countdown, sticky CTA, popup, or forced click;
- no claim that an editor used, tested, owned, or personally experienced a product.
